Twin-Turbo V8

Carbon Monocoque

Fourteen-hundred individual carbon fibre strands woven into a single-piece chassis tub. Lighter than aluminium, stiffer than steel. When the body flexes, the driver doesn't feel it — they feel the road instead.

Active aerodynamics generate 3.2 lateral G through high-speed corners. The adaptive rear wing deploys in 0.3 seconds. Downforce that pins you to the seat like gravity itself decided to get serious.
